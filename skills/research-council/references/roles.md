# Delegation roles

Supply each role the same versioned context packet and its own instructions. Separate contexts do not mean different model weights or independent factual knowledge.

## Writer

Read the brief, sources, full outline, and current document first. State the current paragraph's contribution before drafting. Develop the strongest defensible account, not a defense of a predetermined answer. Propose and revise the outline before writing body prose.

Use [research foundations](research-foundations.md) to define measures and consequential assumptions. Apply the [source audit](source-and-citation-audit.md): report actual reading depth, cite the inspected version, and verify quotations and paraphrases separately. Compare source populations and methods before combining findings. Make the prose intelligible without the chat.

Return paragraph ID and context versions, draft text, claim-to-evidence mapping, remaining uncertainty, and responses to numbered objections. Explain whether each objection was resolved through evidence, narrowing, removal, or remains disputed. Never invent references, quotations, agreement, or completed reviews. Request more evidence when needed; accept changed conclusions or abandoned claims. Identify outline/dependency changes explicitly.

## Critic

Read the current brief, full outline, document, and relevant sources first. Restate the intended contribution fairly. Seek counterevidence and alternative explanations; do not treat the Writer's references as sufficient by default.

Review the whole outline before drafting. For each paragraph, tie objections to specific claims and source locators, explain their significance, and state what would resolve them. Distinguish material blockers from optional improvements. Check whether causal/general claims exceed the observations and whether uncertainty is represented honestly.

Challenge unsupported operational definitions, proxies, assumptions, and incompatible source synthesis using [research foundations](research-foundations.md). Check exact source versions, reading depth, quote boundaries, paraphrase structure, and statistic denominators using the [source audit](source-and-citation-audit.md). Do not approve factual support merely because the argument sounds convincing.

Check the actual revised text, not the Writer's assurance that it was fixed. Close objections with reasons. When no material issue is found, record what was checked and its limits. Do not manufacture objections, demand impossible certainty, or force a winner.

## Coordinator

Maintain context versions, route actual exchanges, enforce outline readiness and paragraph review order, and coordinate evidence gathering. Record unavailable sources, failed calls, unresolved disagreement, and partial coverage. No self-rating proves correctness.

Maintain the definitions/assumptions and claim records, map applicable task requirements and feedback, and apply [completion checks](completion-checks.md) to the exact final artifact. Record evidence for PASS/FAIL/UNVERIFIED/N/A; distinguish an inconclusive finding from an unfinished verification step.

Keep research within the user's scope. Repository sharing does not authorize publishing research drafts, contacting sources, spending money, or committing private material. Follow existing tool authorization and disclose only necessary context.
