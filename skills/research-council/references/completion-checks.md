# Completion checks

Read at task setup for applicable requirements and during final review. The document must stand on its own for its intended reader, without access to the chat.

## Requirements and feedback

Map the actual deliverable requirements to planned sections and observable evidence. For academic work, inspect applicable assignment instructions and AI-assistance rules before drafting. General permission or permission on a different activity does not establish permission for this one. If drafting is prohibited, keep assistance to permitted research, planning, or feedback on user-authored prose. Do not invent personal experience or imply the user performed agent actions.

Read relevant feedback and convert applicable comments into revision checks. Distinguish historical records from current instructions; inaccessible comments do not prove no feedback exists. Do not import course-specific details into unrelated research.

## Standalone and final-artifact review

Define terms and resolve ambiguous pronouns, references, and unstated context. Read the whole argument as a reader who has not seen the discussion. Separate source findings, interpretation, and recommendations; do not manufacture a decisive ending when evidence is inconclusive.

Verify the exact final revision, not an earlier draft. Check citations, bibliography agreement, applicable requirements, and substantive edits. For exported PDF/DOCX or other layout-sensitive output, render and inspect final pages for missing content, clipping, overlap, broken links, headings, and required formatting. A checked source document does not validate an unchecked export. Do not force course-specific formatting on unrelated deliverables.

## Record readiness

Use `PASS`, `FAIL`, `UNVERIFIED`, or `N/A` for each applicable check, with evidence and an explanation. `N/A` requires a reason and cannot conceal missing work.

| Check | Required evidence |
|---|---|
| Task requirements and permitted assistance | Applicable instructions and requirement mapping |
| Foundations and outline | Definitions, consequential assumptions, current reviewed outline |
| Source identity and reading depth | Exact versions and inspected passages/sections |
| Question research file and source collection | Exact dossier/version; search/access log, download status, source summaries, paragraph mapping, dataset calculation feasibility and execution status where applicable |
| Claims and statistics | Claim ledger, units, denominators, source comparisons |
| Quotations, paraphrases, citations | Checked originals, actual locators, bibliography agreement |
| Paragraph discussion | Actual Writer/Critic exchanges and revised-text checks |
| Analysis and standalone explanation | Whole-document review, alternatives, disclosed limits |
| Relevant feedback | Comment-to-revision mapping, or justified N/A |
| Final artifact | Exact filename/version; inspected export when applicable |

PASS requires completed supporting evidence. Do not call the deliverable fully verified or ready while an applicable required check is FAIL or UNVERIFIED. Return partial work with specific remaining checks instead. A completed research report may honestly conclude that a question remains unanswered; disclosed scientific uncertainty differs from unfinished required verification. These checks do not guarantee truth, a grade, or permission to submit, publish, or transmit anything.

In `single-agent approximation`, identify paragraph and final checks as self-review. Such work can satisfy a task that permits that mode, but it cannot receive PASS for a requirement to use separate agents. An unavailable required reviewer remains UNVERIFIED; do not relabel self-review as independent clearance.
